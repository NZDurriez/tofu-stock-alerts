👥 Friends' wishlists

Give someone their own wishlist: choose ＋ Add someone… in your watchlist's dropdown (or add them here), then add things for them. When one comes in stock, they get a Discord ping with checkout links. Nothing opens on this PC.

Their Discord ID: in Discord, Settings → Advanced → turn on Developer Mode, then right-click their name → Copy User ID.

People in your server can keep their own wishlist with your bot's /wishlist command. Each gets a private channel, and drop mode pings them there when something on their list comes in stock at a shop it's watching. Nothing opens on this PC.
